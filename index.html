The Opportunity

Consumers don't lack quotes.
They lack the intelligence to act on them.

The auto insurance shopping experience is structurally broken for consumers. They arrive at comparison sites, get bombarded with spam calls, receive quotes they can't contextualize, and leave more confused than when they started. The platforms that dominate — The Zebra, NerdWallet, Insurify — all optimize for lead monetization first and consumer understanding second. The result is an industry where trust is the scarcest resource.

"Sage is built on a different premise: earn trust through genuine intelligence, then let the purchase follow naturally — with Bindable's quote engine as the seamless final step."

Sage is a carrier intelligence and consumer education platform that fully informs auto insurance decisions before presenting a quote path. It surfaces what consumers actually want to know — which carriers pay claims fastest, which raise rates after one minor accident, which perform best in their state — and then routes ready, educated buyers directly into a licensed quote flow powered by Bindable.

The Shared Problem

The industry is losing most of the funnel before a quote is ever seen.

Through detailed analysis of the current MLP quote flow against the four leading consumer comparison platforms, a clear and addressable gap emerges. This isn't a critique of Bindable's technology — it's a product positioning opportunity that Sage is specifically designed to solve, with Bindable's infrastructure at the center.

~79%

Industry data shows only 21% of consumers who start an insurance quote form complete it to results. The primary culprits: PII asked too early, form length perceived as excessive, and spam anxiety at the contact step. The best performers — Insurify at 25–30% view-to-results — achieve their edge almost entirely through progressive disclosure and explicit no-spam trust architecture. The MLP form, at 17 steps with PII at step 2, sits below the industry average on completion.

The Platform

What Sage is — and where Bindable lives inside it.

01 — Carrier Intelligence Profiles
  • Scored carrier pages: price, claims handling, bind speed, digital experience, regional strength
  • MA-first launch using real RMC/Bindable transaction data — not syndicated ratings
  • Head-to-head comparisons by driver profile: MAPFRE vs. Progressive vs. Travelers for a 35-year-old MA homeowner
  • Sourced from NAIC, DOI filings, AM Best, J.D. Power, and proprietary quote outcomes
02 — Consumer Education Engine
  • Interactive tools: deductible sliders, coverage calculators, premium context benchmarks
  • Directly addresses the 26.2% "Too Expensive" loss: consumers get a frame of reference before they ever see a number
  • No spam, no data selling, no redirect maze — trust architecture built into the product contract
  • Consumers arrive at the quote step already knowing which carrier they want
03 — Quote Concierge, Exclusively Powered by Bindable
  • Consumer selects a carrier after completing the education layer
  • Sage routes them into Bindable's quote and bind flow — pre-qualified, intent-confirmed, and carrier-matched
  • Optimized RAISE form (13 steps, deferred PII, trust architecture) replaces the MLP form for Sage traffic
  • Commission, CPL, and CPA economics preserved — and improved by higher-quality lead entry
